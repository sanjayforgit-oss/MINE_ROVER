
1. Title of the project / product

M.I.N.E.R.S. – Mobile Intelligent Navigation and Emergency Rescue System


2. Problem Statement

Underground mine rescue teams face unknown hazards, poor visibility, difficult terrain, survivor-location uncertainty and unreliable communication, making initial human inspection dangerous and slow.


3. Solution proposed

M.I.N.E.R.S. is a low-cost AI-integrated rescue rover that:

• Monitors CH₄, CO, O₂ and environmental hazards.
• Uses 360° 2D LiDAR for mapping, navigation and obstacle detection.
• Uses one stereo camera for both RGB vision and depth estimation.
• Uses thermal + RGB + depth + posture/motion analysis for survivor detection.
• Builds a dynamic rescue map showing tunnels, hazards, survivors and communication status.
• Uses risk-aware navigation considering hazards, communication and battery level.
• Supports bounded autonomous operation during temporary communication loss.
• Stores mission data locally and synchronizes it after reconnection.
• Provides a live rescue dashboard with video, thermal data, hazards, survivor alerts and rover status.
• Includes an independent Safety Governor, manual override and emergency stop.


4. Implementation Plan

Build and integrate the rover in phases:

Sensors & Drive → ROS 2 → SLAM/Navigation → AI Perception → Risk & Mission Planning → Communication Resilience → Rescue Dashboard → Testing

Target beneficiaries:
Mine rescue teams, underground workers, mine safety officers and emergency response teams.

Expected outcomes:
Safer reconnaissance, faster hazard assessment, improved survivor localization and better rescue-team situational awareness.


5. Safety and Efficiency Analysis

M.I.N.E.R.S. reduces the need for immediate human entry into potentially hazardous areas by performing initial reconnaissance remotely.

Safety:
• Multimodal sensing and sensor redundancy
• Obstacle avoidance and localization monitoring
• Communication-loss safe behaviour
• Battery and fault monitoring
• Independent Safety Governor
• Manual override and emergency stop

Efficiency:
• Rapid underground reconnaissance
• Real-time hazard mapping
• Faster survivor identification
• Centralized rescue information through the dashboard


6. Scalability and Future Development

The modular architecture allows future upgrades such as:

• Industrial-grade communication and relay networks
• Improved thermal and gas sensors
• UWB-assisted localization
• Advanced edge AI and mine-specific datasets
• Multi-rover cooperative exploration
• Improved structural-risk analysis
• Certified mine-deployable hardware
• Adaptation for tunnels, disaster zones and confined-space inspection


7. Innovation Advantage

M.I.N.E.R.S. does not rely on a single AI model or sensor. Its key innovation is the integration of:

Multimodal Survivor Intelligence
+
Risk-Aware Navigation
+
Communication-Resilient Bounded Autonomy
+
Dynamic Rescue Intelligence Map
+
Independent Safety Governor

During communication loss, the rover can continue a predefined bounded mission, store information locally and attempt safe recovery/reconnection instead of immediately losing the mission.

This creates a unified rescue-support system rather than a conventional inspection rover with isolated sensing capabilities.
